# Roadmap

Planned for the next version:

- **Group totals in the tree.** Collapsed app rows show the combined memory and CPU of the app and all its helpers, e.g. "Claude, 1.4 GB total".
- **Reopen after a clean.** After Quick Clean or Fresh Start, one click relaunches the apps that were quit.
- **History sparklines and leak detection.** A 5-minute CPU and memory history for the selected process. Purge flags processes whose memory keeps growing.
- **Launch at login and a global hotkey.** Start with your Mac, and show or hide the pinned window from anywhere.

Later:

- Signed and notarized download, so you don't have to build from source
- Window transparency slider, from fully opaque down to 50%, so a pinned window can sit over your work
- Lower idle CPU by updating only the rows that changed
